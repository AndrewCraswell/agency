Fencing Club

Fencing Club is a specialty retailer of Olympic fencing equipment and apparel, serving fencing clubs, schools, and individual athletes. We have been in business since 2023 and are based in the United States.

We focus exclusively on fencing, with an emphasis on dependable equipment, fair pricing, and clear support for athletes from beginner through competitive levels.

Our background

Based in Woodinville, in the Seattle metropolitan area, Fencing Club began after extensive travel to fencing clubs and training facilities around the world. Through these visits, we saw firsthand how challenging it can be for athletes and clubs to obtain proper fencing gear, whether due to limited availability, inconsistent quality, or high costs driven by long supply chains.

These experiences shaped our commitment to ensure that cost and access are never barriers to participation in fencing. From beginners just starting out to competitive fencers training at higher levels, many athletes struggle to find reliable equipment that meets their needs.

Fencing Club was created to provide a dedicated, dependable place where athletes at every stage can acquire quality fencing gear, supported by direct sourcing, clear guidance, and straightforward service.

Sourcing and operations

We work directly with manufacturing partners and established fencing brands around the world to source reliable equipment and maintain consistent quality.

Fencing Club operates as a direct retailer and maintains its own inventory at our facility. Orders are shipped from U.S. based fulfillment locations. In some cases, we use third party logistics providers as fulfillment partners. These partners support shipping and delivery only and do not act as resellers.

All customer service, order support, and returns are handled directly by Fencing Club.
