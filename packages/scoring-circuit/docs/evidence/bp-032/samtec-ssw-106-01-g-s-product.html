SSW-106-01-G-S - .100in/2.54mm Socket Strip Receptacle Solder Tail, 6 Pin, 1 Row, 20µin Gold/Flash, .104in Tail

Printed circuit board socket strips on a .100" pitch have been a staple of the electronics industry for decades. Samtec has brought them up to date with the high retention, cost-effective Tiger Buy™ contact. Available in surface mount and through-hole, these versatile sockets are ideal for new designs and retrofit applications.

Download Product Specs

Features

  • Pitch: .100" (2.54 mm)

  • Application: Single, double and triple row up to 150 pins

  • Up to 8 Gbps performance

  • Contact System: Tiger Buy™

  • Orientation: Vertical, right-angle

  • Termination: Through-hole, surface mount

  • Special Features: Standard and low insertion force contacts with standard solder tails

  • Automotive A-Series product available

How is this calculated?

Protocol capability of Samtec connectors results from a comparison of the protocol channel data rate and the Samtec Channel Performance Metric (CPM) per product. The Samtec CPM estimates connector performance in a pre-defined channel as illustrated below.

This channel-based approach factors impairments—such as the crosstalk and reflections—inherent in real-world signal channels. Samtec CPM sums all channel noise sources and then determines the resultant signal to noise ratio in a specific mated connector pair. By its very nature, CPM is a function of channel assumptions made and will vary from application to application. Achieving similar results requires good SI design practice in the system PCB(s) and using Samtec-provided connector breakout regions (BOR).

The SSW series has a CPM of 8 Gbps NRZ.

SSW products are capable of protocols whose channel data rates are less than this CPM.

For more information, please read this blog article: A New High Speed Connector Rating That Accounts For The Entire System and download the Samtec Channel Performance Metric white paper.
