# Application V3_3 rail

## Decision and boundary

The application `V3_3` rail is now represented by a concrete regulator
candidate: Texas Instruments `LMR43620MSC3RPERQ1`. It is an active 2 A fixed
3.3 V automotive buck orderable in the 2 mm x 2 mm, 9-pin RPE VQFN-HR package.
The device is selected because its guaranteed fixed-output range is 3.27 V to
3.33 V over line, load, and temperature in FPWM mode. The executable budget
now carries that 3.27 V minimum through explicit DC distribution and transient
allowances to the `TPS389033DSER` SENSE/VDD pins and the ESP32 module 3V3 pins.
It fails closed if either the supervisor release margin, ESP32 regulated margin,
or latest-assertion margin reaches zero.

This is a schematic-level candidate and closure calculation, not a released
schematic, routed PCB, thermal sign-off, or fabrication approval. Effective
ceramic capacitance, V5 regulation, output transient response, inductor
temperature, regulator junction temperature, and reset timing still require
bench evidence.

## Power path

`V5` is the upstream rail generated by the selected USB-PD system converter.
The application converter receives only `V5`, not raw USB-C VBUS or the
negotiated 20 V rail. The design screen uses 4.75 V to 5.25 V at the
`LMR43620` VIN pin, leaving 1.15 V above the device's 3.6 V startup minimum.
The VIN and EN/UVLO pins are tied to V5. The fixed-frequency
`MODE/SYNC` pin is tied to the device VCC pin, selecting FPWM with spread
spectrum; this avoids the approximately 1% no-load offset associated with
auto mode.

| Reference | Selected part or value | Function and footprint intent |

| `U_APP_REGULATOR` | TI `LMR43620MSC3RPERQ1` | Fixed 3.3 V, 2 A buck; RPE VQFN-HR, 2 mm x 2 mm, 9 pins |

| `C_APP_REG_IN` | TDK `C2012X7R1E475K125AB` | 4.7 uF, 25 V, X7R, 0805 bulk input bypass |

The three 22 uF capacitors have 66 uF nameplate capacitance. The design
calculation requires at least 40 uF effective capacitance after DC-bias,
temperature, tolerance, and assembly effects. The bank must be checked using
the manufacturer's bias curves and then measured on the assembled board.

## Worst-case calculation

The calculation in `src/application-rail.ts` uses the existing USB-PD budget's
local 3.3 V allocations of 2.60 W continuous and 3.20 W peak, a conservative
0.85 efficiency floor, the TI 84.4 C/W JESD thermal value, and a 50 C blocked-
vent ambient screen. The result is:

| Check | Continuous | Peak |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| Local output current using 3.27 V minimum | 0.80 A | 0.98 A |
| Input current at 4.75 V and 0.85 efficiency | 0.65 A | 0.80 A |
| Regulator dissipation at 0.85 efficiency | 0.46 W | 0.56 W |
| Estimated junction at 50 C and 84.4 C/W | 89 C | 98 C |

The LMR43620-Q1 minimum high-side peak current limit is 2.8 A. At the peak
load, the 2.2 uH inductor contributes about 0.21 A ripple at the minimum V5
screen. Including half the ripple, the regulator current margin is more than
1.7 A. The inductor peak current with a further 20% transient margin is about
1.30 A, below its 5.0 A 20%-saturation rating and 8.7 A 40 C-rise rating.

## Pin-level voltage budget

The supervisor is Kelvin-routed to the application point-of-load region. The
calculation reserves 5 mV DC distribution loss and 15 mV transient droop from
the regulator minimum to the supervisor SENSE/VDD pins. It independently
reserves 20 mV DC distribution loss and 50 mV transient droop to the ESP32 3V3
pins. At brownout assertion, a further 10 mV maximum supervisor-to-ESP32 DC
mismatch plus the 50 mV local transient allowance is charged against the
latest possible supervisor assertion threshold. These values are design
limits to verify on the routed board, not measured facts.

| Limit | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Supervisor release threshold, worst high | 3.189 V x 1.01 | 3.2209 V |
| Regulator output, worst low | Datasheet fixed-output limit | 3.2700 V |
| Supervisor pin minimum | 3.2700 V - 5 mV DC - 15 mV transient | 3.2500 V |
| Supervisor release margin | 3.2500 V - 3.2209 V | 29.1 mV |
| ESP32 regulated pin minimum | 3.2700 V - 20 mV DC - 50 mV transient | 3.2000 V |
| ESP32 regulated margin | 3.2000 V - 3.0000 V module minimum | 200.0 mV |
| Supervisor assert threshold, latest | 3.170 V x 0.99 | 3.1383 V |
| ESP32 pin at latest assertion | 3.1383 V - 10 mV mismatch - 50 mV transient | 3.0783 V |
| ESP32 latest-assertion margin | 3.0783 V - 3.0000 V module minimum | 78.3 mV |

The threshold comparison alone does not guarantee assertion during every rail
collapse because TI does not specify a maximum falling SENSE-to-RESET
propagation delay. Brownout slew-rate and RESET/EN_RESET scope tests therefore
remain mandatory.

## Guaranteed reset-delay screen

The selected `C0603C104K3RACTU` CT capacitor is 100 nF, +/-10%, X7R with a
manufacturer-stated +/-15% temperature coefficient and 3% aging per decade
hour. The executable screen reserves another 20% after tolerance and
temperature for DC bias and lifetime aging, producing 61.2 nF effective
minimum. TPS3890 Equation 1 is evaluated with the fastest specified charge
corner: 1.17 V minimum CT threshold and 1.35 uA maximum CT current. The
datasheet gives only a nominal 25 us CT-open propagation delay, so the
guaranteed-minimum calculation takes zero baseline-delay credit.

| Reset-delay limit | Calculation | Result |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Effective CT minimum | 100 nF x 0.90 x 0.85 x 0.80 | 61.2 nF |
| Guaranteed delay minimum | 61.2 nF x 1.17 V / 1.35 uA + 0 ms | 53.04 ms |
| Nominal reference only | 100 nF x 1.23 V / 1.15 uA + 0.025 ms | 106.98 ms |
| Required hold | 4.6 ms soft start + 10 ms settling + 0.05 ms Espressif minimum | 14.65 ms |
| Guaranteed delay margin | 53.04 ms - 14.65 ms | 38.39 ms |

The 10 ms settling interval is an engineering requirement beyond Espressif's
50 us minimum power-stable-before-CHIP_PU interval. The assembled CT value and
actual delay still require lot, voltage, and temperature measurements.

The 1 kOhm discharge resistor draws 3.3 mA at nominal V3_3. With 40 uF
effective output capacitance it reaches 10% of the rail in approximately
92 ms after the regulator is disabled. This is a discharge aid only; the
supervisor and ESP32 load behavior must be measured during power removal.

## Release gates

1. Verify the upstream V5 rail at the application regulator pins across the
   full USB-PD source, load, temperature, cable-drop, and brownout envelope.
2. Import and compare the TI RPE land pattern, exposed pad, stencil, and
   thermal copper against the exact package drawing before PCB release.
3. Import and independently verify the exact Coilcraft XGL4030 recommended
   land pattern, including terminal geometry, solder mask, paste, courtyard,
   and pin mapping. The circuit intentionally emits no inductor pads and marks
   the part do-not-place until this gate closes; a generic `0402` or other
   passive footprint is prohibited.
4. Verify the effective capacitance of the output bank at 3.3 V bias and the
   full temperature range. Reject any lot or substitution that cannot retain
   40 uF effective capacitance.
5. Scope cold startup, V5 removal, V5 droop, regulator current limit, output
   discharge, supervisor reset release, brownout assertion, and ESP32 EN_RESET
   at the worst load. Verify at least 53.04 ms release delay and characterize
   assertion versus the rail-collapse slope.
6. Measure load-step response from the documented continuous envelope to the
   100 ms peak envelope, including V3_3 minimum and reset false-release.
7. Measure regulator, inductor, capacitor, and adjacent-board temperatures at
   50 C blocked-vent ambient. The calculation does not close thermal or EMC
   validation.
8. Complete ERC, placement, return-path, switch-node, EMI, DRC, and independent
   power-review gates before calling the application rail fabrication-ready.
9. Measure the regulator output, supervisor SENSE/VDD, and ESP32 3V3 pins
   simultaneously. Reject routing or loading that exceeds 5 mV supervisor DC,
   15 mV supervisor transient, 20 mV ESP32 DC, 50 mV ESP32 transient, or 10 mV
   supervisor-to-ESP32 mismatch budgets.
