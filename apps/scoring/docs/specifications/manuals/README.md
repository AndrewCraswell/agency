# Commercial scoring-machine manual corpus

**Snapshot date:** 2026-08-25

This directory preserves the official manuals and directly relevant technical notes used for the commercial feature
catalog. The scope is Favero's current fencing scoring apparatus families and their directly connected repeaters, the
Virtual Scoring Machine, and the Skewered Fencing scoring-box manual. It does not include unrelated products such as
multisport scoreboards.

The files are evidence about reference products, not normative requirements. The
[FIE Material Rules](../fie-material-rules-2026-08-en.pdf) remain authoritative for competition scoring behavior. A
manufacturer's claim of FIE timing, compatibility, or homologation is recorded as a claim unless the corresponding
official certificate and current rules establish it.

## Extraction and citation rules

- Cite the local filename and PDF page number for PDF evidence. For multilingual PDFs, identify the English section.
- Cite the heading and snapshot date for the Skewered HTML manual.
- Record `advertised` when a feature is present only on a product page; do not silently promote it to `documented`.
- Preserve the original files byte-for-byte. Derived text extraction and rendered pages belong under `tmp/pdfs/`, not
  in this evidence corpus.
- A feature appearing in a manual does not establish FIE conformance, electrical compliance, or interoperability.
