# FIE scoring apparatus traceability matrix

M0-01 baseline contract for the three-weapon scoring apparatus.

## Authority, scope, and notation

The normative source for this matrix is the local copy of **FIE Material Rules,
Book 3, August 2026**, in
[`fie-material-rules-2026-08-en.pdf`](fie-material-rules-2026-08-en.pdf). The
file is the downloaded source identified by `apps/scoring/docs/README.md` and
has SHA-256 `1489D28ED6F3C91E27ECDF75BB29B4ED65C688A012F544D37D946A9DA81AFC26`.
Page references below are the printed page numbers in that PDF, not a viewer's
zero-based page index.

The order of authority is:

1. The cited article or Annex B paragraph in the FIE PDF is normative.
2. A project contract may choose a deterministic point inside an FIE tolerance,
   but must record that choice and must not describe it as a new FIE limit.
3. Bench measurements, homologation tests, and venue trials provide evidence
   of the implementation; they do not change the rule.
4. The Favero FA-15 documents in this folder are **prior art only**. They are
   comparison and test-fixture references, never normative authority. A Favero
   value must not be copied into a rule table unless the FIE citation in the
   same row independently supports it.

`Valid`, `non-valid`, `fault`, and `no signal` below are electrical apparatus
outcomes. Referee interpretation, fencing priority, and match scoring remain
outside the STM32 electrical decision unless a later contract explicitly says
otherwise.

### Foil

| ID | Normative requirement and exact source | Implementation and evidence mapping | Unresolved interpretation or gate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| FOIL-01 | Foil registers when the permanently circulating foil-circuit current is broken. An on-target hit gives red or green; an off-target hit gives white. The sound is a short ring or a note automatically limited to 2 seconds, identical for both sides. **FIE Annex B, A.1(a).1-A.1(a).3, pp. 77-78.** | M1-03 defines the foil contact-break state machine and output event types. M0-05 maps `on-target`, `off-target`, and rejection records to immutable evidence. M5-04 and M8-03 verify each lamp and buzzer channel. | Define the polarity and idle state of the foil circuit in M0-03. Do not derive a target classification from a single ADC threshold without a declared line state and uncertainty outcome. |
| FOIL-02 | A break of 14 ms +/- 1 ms must always signal. Valid-hit registration is guaranteed for a 13-15 ms break with exterior resistance 0-500 ohms; at 14 ms +/- 1 ms it must signal for 0-200 ohms. The apparatus may show valid only, valid plus non-valid, or non-valid only as resistance increases; valid/non-valid cases require resistance below 500 ohms. **FIE Annex B, A.1(b).1-A.1(b).3, pp. 78-79.** | Store time in integer microseconds and test below, at, and above every boundary: 13, 14, and 15 ms, plus 0, 200, and 500 ohms. Planned evidence: M1-03, M1-04, M1-08, M4-01, M4-05, M4-08, and M6-05. | FIE gives a tolerance and guaranteed regions but does not prescribe the internal sampling or quantisation method. M1-04 must choose conservative acceptance guard bands and represent measurement uncertainty explicitly. |
| FOIL-03 | The apparatus tolerates up to 200 ohms added resistance in the closed foil circuit without a non-valid signal. With foil earth resistance up to 100 ohms it must not signal guard/piste hits or a hit caused merely by touching the conductive jacket with a blade or point without depressing it. When blades touch, valid and non-valid hits must register normally regardless of resistance between blades. **FIE Annex B, A.1(b).4-A.1(b).6, pp. 78-79.** | Add closed-loop, earth-fault, blade-contact, and no-depression cases to the seven-line front-end model. Planned evidence: M1-03, M1-08, M2-02, M4-01, M4-08, M4-09, and M6-05. | "Up to" resistance is a test limit, not permission to clamp or normalise an out-of-range line. Out-of-range or contradictory readings must be diagnostic faults and must not silently score. |
| FOIL-04 | An anti-blocking apparatus must retain the above requirements except the blade-contact point, continue to register valid and non-valid hits despite a fencer's own weapon-to-jacket insulation short, register a valid hit up to 200 ohms and non-valid hits above 200 ohms in the opponent's return circuit, and provide two yellow lamps: on below 450 ohms and never on above 475 ohms. Yellow lamps indicate insulation faults only; a lit lamp requires the Referee to stop the bout and call technical experts. They need not indicate jacket-to-piste contact. **FIE Annex B, A.2, p. 79.** | Represent anti-blocking as a declared apparatus mode, not an accidental side effect. Record yellow-lamp state and the resistance bucket as a diagnostic event. Planned evidence: M0-03, M1-04, M2-04, M4-01, M4-08, M5-04, M6-05, and M7-03. | The 450-475 ohm band is intentionally not assigned a lamp result by FIE. M1-04 must define it as an uncertainty/diagnostic state or a validated hysteresis policy; it must not claim a normative threshold inside the band. |
| FOIL-05 | After any hit, on or off target, the same side cannot register a subsequent hit. No priority is indicated. After 300 ms +/- 25 ms from the first signalled hit, all later signals are ignored. **FIE Annex B, A.1(a).4-A.1(a).6, p. 78.** General hit lamps remain until reset. **FIE m.51.3-4, p. 44.** | The foil state machine needs per-side inhibit plus a bout-level event window. Boundary vectors cover 275, 300, and 325 ms and same-side versus opposite-side events. Planned evidence: M1-03, M1-08, M1-09, M2-04, and M6-05. | "First hit signalled" is the apparatus event time, not necessarily the physical contact start. M1-03 must freeze that timestamp rule and distinguish electrical lockout from referee priority. |

| SABRE-02 | Two yellow lamps indicate own guard/blade contact with the fencer's conductive equipment. | SABRE-04 | If a fault down to 0 ohms leaks current between a fencer's valid surface and weapon, all exchanged hits remain registerable; yellow indicates the faulty side at 0-450 ohms. | SABRE-05 | After a hit has registered, a hit by the other fencer is registered only within a maximum of 170 ms (+/- 10 ms). | SABRE-06 | With contact through the blade, register between 0 and 4 ms (+1 ms); prevent registration between 4 and 15 ms (+5 ms) if blade contact is interrupted no more than 10 times. shared README. The next rule-dependent work is M0-02, M0-03, M0-04, M0-05,
