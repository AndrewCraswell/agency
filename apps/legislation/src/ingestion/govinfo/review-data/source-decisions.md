## Historical assignments and quarantine

Brown and Spence were explicitly retained as historical information in the source directories. The notes occur in the
full PDFs for `CDIR-1999-06-15` and `CDIR-2000-02-01` (page 3), and `CDIR-2001-12-07` and `CDIR-2002-10-01` (page 1).
Retrospective compilation notes cannot be backdated to a package issue date. Preserve unknown observed/effective dates
using the historical-at-first-observation contract; do not invent a departure or zero-length tenure.

The October 2022 Senate Appropriations Tom Udall/NM cell conflicts with his recorded Senate term. The
[official House biography](https://history.house.gov/People/Detail/20879?ret=True) corroborates the contradiction. The
exact reviewed cell is quarantined, not replaced by another senator. The 117th roster remains explicitly incomplete.
Changed edition/context/fingerprint requires a new review; this exception never authorizes general partial publication.
