# Pricing and offerings

Starting proposal recovered from the September 14, 2026 product discussion, including the subsequent decision to
remove consumption quotas. These are prices to test for completed features, not launched subscriptions or validated demand.
The [initial market research](../research/pricing.md) contains the competitor plans, source links and comparison limits.

## Starting tiers

| Plan | Monthly | Annual | Core offering |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | $0 | $0 | Personal discovery, legislative records, source inspection, basic following and introductory research |
| Professional | $79 | $790 | Full personal research: persistent issues, conversations, comparisons, reusable briefs and configurable alerts |
| Firm | $299 per organization | $2,990 per organization | Shared client/initiative workspaces, assignments, review, branded reports, controlled sharing and organization administration |
| Enterprise | Annual contract | Custom | Advanced access management, automated provisioning, retention controls, contracted integrations, onboarding and support |

USD. Annual prices are the initial proposal, approximately 16.7% below twelve monthly payments. Firm also serves
companies, unions and lobbying teams; the label does not restrict the buyer to a law firm. Included seats and additional
member pricing remain undecided. The earlier $15,000/year enterprise starting point is an unapproved hypothesis, not a floor.

## Free boundaries

| Included | Paid boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Search and inspect released legislative records across verified coverage | No artificial state restriction or stale free dataset |
| Source text, citations, versions and coverage disclosures | Never hide evidence quality or known gaps behind payment |
| Basic personal following | Advanced alert configuration and organization delivery are paid |
| Introductory research, comparison and brief previews | Persistent issue management, saved comparisons and reusable briefs are Professional |
| Private personal account | Shared workspaces, assignments, team review and organization administration are Firm |
| Inspect/copy source references | Branded Word/PDF reports and controlled client report sharing are Firm |

The exact introductory AI experience remains unresolved. Before launch, define the available actions and persistence
clearly; do not ship the phrase "introductory research" as the sole entitlement explanation. Basic following also needs
an explicit event/cadence specification. These decisions must use feature boundaries rather than reinstating question quotas.
Free does not include enterprise provisioning, retention policies, custom integrations or contracted support.

## Pricing principles

- Start flat and differentiate by completed workflow and organization capabilities.
- No published question, bill, issue, saved-query or generated-response caps initially; no response top-ups or automatic overages.
- Measure consumption internally. Ordinary abuse, concurrency and runaway-job protections are operational controls;
  disclose any restriction that materially affects legitimate use. Avoid a permanent promise of unlimited consumption.
- Keep source inspection, account isolation, privacy and trustworthy coverage information in every tier.
- WorkOS login already exists. Secure login is not the enterprise upgrade; provisioning, policy and application access
  controls are additional work. API, webhooks and MCP retain their home in Settings, Integrations.
- Source-data redistribution and bespoke integration contracts need explicit rights and scope. Developer-feed packaging
  and tier placement of API/MCP/webhooks remain to be decided; existing endpoints do not establish commercial entitlements.

The original response/issue/query quotas, five-seat Firm bundle, $39 additional seats and twenty-seat enterprise
package are not current decisions. Do not reintroduce them through mockups, billing code or the archived discussion.

## Organization and enterprise delivery

The [organization specification](organization-features.md) owns feature behavior and design placement: issue workspaces,
assignments, review, report branding, Word/PDF exports, named report recipients, email digests, Slack/Teams and organization
settings. A client initially sees approved shared reports, not internal drafts or unrelated workspaces.

Enterprise contracts should specify organization scope, member policy, provisioning, access controls, audit exports,
retention/deletion, integration ownership, onboarding, support hours and renewal terms. Availability and response targets
must be supported by actual monitoring and staffing. Application uptime, source publication, ingestion and alert delivery
are separate promises. No uptime percentage, certification, data-residency guarantee or 24/7 support is approved here.

## Measurement and launch decisions

Measure model/retrieval cost, ingestion and licensed-data allocation, storage, monitoring, notification delivery,
authentication, support and payment costs per account and plan. Separate shared corpus cost from account-driven work.
Track typical and expensive legitimate accounts, legislative-session peaks, free-account cost and conversion.
Recognize annual revenue over twelve months when comparing cost with revenue. Change pricing based on measured economics
and customer outcomes; an old OCR repair cohort is not a complete cost model.

Before launch, resolve Free's exact research/following boundaries, Firm seats, API/MCP/webhook entitlements, enterprise
minimum economics and billing/cancellation policy. Test Professional and Firm through paid pilots using real recurring
research and reviewed reports. Personal plans require the personal workflow and billing lifecycle; Firm requires actual
shared ownership and report permissions; Enterprise requires the promised controls and service. Regulatory coverage is
future scope and must not be sold as delivered because it appears in a research plan.
